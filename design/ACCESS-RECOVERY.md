Use only eligible current public listings, not stock replacements. Two valid items are sufficient; do not fabricate a third. Fetch independently of authentication so loading, empty inventory and failure never disable access or lose form state. A dedicated state board shows skeletons, genuine emptiness and retry after failure. A failed fetch is not an empty-feed claim. Missing covers use an explicit placeholder; long titles and areas need truncation/wrapping within stable card bounds. ## Contract

- Primary authentication remains email and password. Apple Pay / Google Pay belong to payments and do not add social sign-in. No phone login, magic-link access, passkeys or MFA is introduced.
- Registration collects a 2–50-character public display name, private primary email, password, age-18+ declaration and current Terms/Privacy acceptance. No birth date, username, institution or avatar is required here. Both consent controls are unchecked fixtures. Their entire rows should be keyboard/touch targets, with separate legal links.
- Passwords are 10–128 characters without invented composition rules. Email is validated against feature 002's normalization and length contract. Eye controls have a 44 px target, use accessible Show/Hide labels and preserve focus/caret. Support password managers and paste; never put passwords/tokens in URLs, logs or analytics.
- User-requested refinement: registration and new-password forms now include a second confirmation field. Compare exactly, without trimming or transforming either entry. Show mismatch only after appropriate interaction, clear it as the values match, and require equality before submitting. The confirmation is transient UI state, never a new stored credential or telemetry value. Both fields support autofill/paste and independent show/hide controls. Reconcile this added UI validation in an approved implementation task before coding.
- New-password creation/reset include a neutral initial strength meter, text label and four segments. It updates from a locally evaluated estimate of length and predictability, not simply a count of satisfied character classes. Its optional uppercase/lowercase/number/symbol indicators reflect what was typed; they are guidance, not mandatory green ticks. The state board explicitly illustrates a predictable password with all varieties still rated weak and a long uncommon phrase rated strong without all varieties. These are fictional visual states, not results of an implemented estimator. Do not transmit raw passwords to external scoring services or persist/cache/log them. Avoid percentages, crack-time promises or guarantees of security; the bar is advisory and does not introduce a new strength threshold into the current contract.
- The existing 10–128 bound is unchanged by this artwork. The no-composition-rule rationale is supported by [NIST SP 800-63B-4](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63b.html#passwordver): character-type requirements are discouraged and long phrases are supported. This is not a claim of full NIST conformity: that publication also specifies a 15-character minimum for single-factor passwords. Any change to the approved project's minimum, blocklist or strength gate needs its own reconciliation rather than being silently added here.
- Primary-email confirmation is necessary to participate, while public browsing remains open. University verification is a different, optional flow and does not gate ordinary account creation.
- Registration and recovery instruction screens use conditional, non-identifying responses rather than claiming account existence or confirmed email delivery. Sign-in failure remains generic, including unconfirmed/invalid/unavailable credentials. Delivery or transport failures cannot render a false confirmed/completed outcome.
- Resend/request-another is bounded by server eligibility, not an invented countdown. Rate-limit artwork is illustrative: the live form must show the actual allowed retry time and remain disabled while ineligible. Repeated clicks during submission are disabled.
- `Use another email` returns to the relevant email-entry form; it is not a feature to change an existing account's primary email. Preserve safe input/context as appropriate without exposing private addresses in instruction screens. Switching locale preserves intended destination and safe form input, without logging or persisting credentials.
- One-time confirmation links and recovery links reject invalid/expired/used tokens. The existing specification uses 24-hour confirmation and 30-minute recovery lifetimes. This design does not change those values or implement a token.
- Email-confirmed and password-updated success appear only after acknowledged success. Reset revokes previous sessions and returns to sign-in, not an automatic session. There is no invented Remember me control; current bounded session semantics remain.
- Authentication return destinations must be validated same-origin local views. After sign-in, return to the intended listing, message or reservation view; never automatically send a message, accept an offer or charge a payment that was pending before authentication. The session-expiry board's reservation destination is a fictional safe local context.

Legal wording follows the current approved identity requirement; final legal copy/review is separate from these static layouts. Terms/privacy/Impressum labels must route to the existing appropriate documents. Account/session management and deletion are the next unit rather than hidden inside the access forms.

State-board cards are behavior illustrations and do not substitute for complete field-error or modal variants.

Expanded mobile form artboards represent scrollable content, not a claim that every field fits one viewport. Repositioned desktop artboards to avoid overlap. Static designs only; no login, password processing, live feed subscription or database change was implemented.
