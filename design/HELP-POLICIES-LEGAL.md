## Help and contact behavior

Pickup guidance reflects the existing policy: arrange through the listing conversation, choose a busy public place where practical, inspect before paying, avoid advance deposits and report/block concerns. It does not introduce official meetup spots, shipping, platform-held funds or guarantees. The rules overview is an editorial summary, not the full enforceable policy or statutory terms. A complete public policy destination and version must be approved and wired before publication; do not present the overview as an exhaustive prohibited-items list.

The badge FAQ preserves optional participation, TU Braunschweig-only institutional inbox confirmation, private email and twelve-calendar-month validity. The reporting guide states that both participants cannot send after a block, the other participant sees a generic unavailable state, and unblocking doesn't withdraw a report or bypass reciprocal restrictions. No quick replies, silent message dropping or new evidence access is introduced.

Contact is an email path, not a ticket form, chat service or contact-submission backend. There is no sent receipt, response-time promise, reference number, attachment control or automatic inclusion of private conversations. Email CampusMarkt / Email privacy contact must resolve the existing public contact configuration, with a readable/copyable real address in the final integrated UI as fallback. The design does not invent an email address or render the source's `.local` development fallbacks as usable production contacts. Actual public destinations need confirmation/configuration before launch; no outbound email was sent. Listing/account concerns return users to the existing target-specific report action.

Help topic links are navigation proposals, not new implemented search/category endpoints. No search across support content, AI assistance or article helpfulness tracking was added. The detailed publishing/photo and account-access article collections remain content work; use the existing screen flows as the contract. The “Buying, selling & pickup” topic can open the pickup guide in this first layout.
