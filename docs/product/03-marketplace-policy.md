# CampusMarkt Marketplace Policy

## Purpose

CampusMarkt exists for safe, local transfer of ordinary physical goods. This policy defines the baseline product rules for listings, user conduct, privacy, reports, and moderation.

This is a product policy, not a complete statement of German or European law. Before public launch, legal review must reconcile the policy with current consumer, platform, privacy, youth-protection, and prohibited-goods requirements. If law is stricter than this document, the law controls.

## Participation

- Anyone can browse public marketplace content.
- A registered account is required to publish, favorite, message, submit purchase intent, make an offer, reserve, complete, or report.
- University verification is optional. It is a trust signal, not a gate to standard marketplace functions.
- Users must represent themselves and their goods honestly.
- Users must not evade enforcement by creating replacement accounts or reposting removed content.
- Users must not use CampusMarkt for harassment, threats, fraud, discrimination, spam, or unlawful activity.

Feature specs define minimum age, account eligibility, business-seller handling, and any identity checks before registration is implemented. These points are not silently inferred from the student positioning.

## Allowed V1 Content

A V1 listing must concern one lawful physical good that the listing owner is entitled to offer or seek through a supported intent:

- `SELL`: offer a physical good for an exact asking price in euros.
- `GIVE_AWAY`: offer a physical good for free.
- `WANTED`: seek a physical good.

Examples of intended categories include furniture, household goods, kitchenware, books, study supplies, bicycles, ordinary electronics, clothing, and other lawful personal goods. A category does not override this policy; a prohibited item remains prohibited even if it fits a visible category.

## Prohibited Content

Prohibited means CampusMarkt does not permit the item or activity because of safety, legal, privacy, exploitation, or marketplace-integrity risk. It is different from a legitimate capability that the product has not implemented yet.

### Weapons and violence

- Firearms, ammunition, explosives, and weapon components.
- Knives or objects marketed or configured as weapons.
- Instructions, materials, or devices intended to harm people, animals, or property.

### Drugs, medication, alcohol, and nicotine

- Illegal drugs and drug-production equipment.
- Prescription, restricted, recalled, or improperly labeled medication.
- Alcohol.
- Tobacco, nicotine, vaping products, and related regulated consumables.

### Stolen, counterfeit, and unlawful goods

- Stolen goods or goods the user is not entitled to transfer.
- Counterfeit goods, forged documents, and unauthorized replicas presented as genuine.
- Products or transactions that infringe intellectual-property or other rights.
- Items whose sale, possession, import, or transfer is unlawful.

### Dangerous and regulated materials

- Explosives, fireworks, toxic substances, hazardous chemicals, radioactive material, and unsafe batteries.
- Recalled products or goods known to present a serious safety risk.
- Human remains, bodily materials, or regulated medical devices that cannot lawfully be transferred through this marketplace.

### People, animals, and exploitation

- Animals and animal sales or transfers.
- Sexual services, explicit adult goods, or exploitative content.
- Human trafficking, labor exploitation, or any offer involving control over a person.

### Privacy, access, and deceptive content

- Personal data, private documents, identity documents, access credentials, or financial information.
- Hacked, compromised, locked, or fraudulently obtained devices and accounts.
- Misleading listings, impersonation, phishing, advance-fee schemes, or off-platform payment fraud.
- Content designed primarily to redirect users to another business, marketplace, or referral scheme.

Moderation can remove a listing that creates a comparable risk even when this document does not name the exact item. A recurring category-level decision must be added to the policy rather than enforced as an invisible rule.

## Unsupported Content

Unsupported means the concept may be lawful but CampusMarkt V1 does not provide the required product model, policy, or transaction flow.

- Services and freelance work.
- Jobs and recruiting.
- Housing, rooms, sublets, and property.
- Digital goods, downloadable files, software licenses, keys, and user accounts.
- Tickets and reservations.
- Rentals and lending.
- Shipping-only listings.
- Auctions.
- `SWAP` or barter listings.
- Financial products, loans, fundraising, investments, and cryptocurrency transactions.
- Vehicles or other regulated categories that require a dedicated policy not yet approved.

Unsupported content must not be hidden under a goods category. Future verticals receive their own models, policies, and feature specifications before launch.

## Listing Integrity

Listing requirements will be finalized by the listing feature. The policy baseline is:

- Use an accurate title, description, type, condition, category, price, and pickup area.
- Show the actual good offered. Do not use misleading stock images or conceal material defects.
- Create one clear listing for the good or explicitly approved bundle.
- Do not keyword-spam, duplicate active listings, or manipulate discovery.
- Do not publish private contact details, precise home addresses, payment credentials, or institutional email addresses.
- Do not mark a monetary sale as `GIVE_AWAY` to evade price or transaction rules.
- Do not request deposits, gift cards, cryptocurrency, or other hard-to-recover payment before a V1 pickup.

## Negotiation and Local Pickup

- Purchase intent and offers must use the structured marketplace action when available.
- A chat message alone does not reserve a listing or change its official price.
- An accepted purchase intent or offer can create one active reservation.
- Users must not pressure others into a different price, payment method, or unsafe meeting location.
- For V1, payment is arranged outside CampusMarkt. CampusMarkt does not secure, hold, release, or refund funds.
- Meet in a busy public place where practical.
- Inspect the item before paying.
- Do not enter an unfamiliar private location when a safe public alternative is available.
- Keep relevant communication in CampusMarkt where possible so participants can understand what was agreed.
- CampusMarkt Meetup Spots are not available until a dedicated feature verifies and maintains official locations.

## Privacy

### Public by design

- Approved profile display fields.
- Listing content and coarse pickup area.
- Current university name and verification badge when the user chooses verification and the badge is valid.

### Private by design

- Primary account email.
- Institutional verification email or its masked local part.
- Verification tokens and durable email hashes.
- Password, session, recovery, and authentication data.
- Private messages and exact pickup arrangements.
- Precise home address or continuous location.
- Reporter's identity relative to the reported user.
- Moderator notes and internal risk signals.

Private data is accessible only to the user, authorized participants, or authorized operators for a defined purpose. Logging must not copy message bodies, raw institutional emails, secrets, or precise pickup information into ordinary telemetry.

## University Verification

- Verification uses an institutional address separate from the primary account email.
- The institutional address is used only for verification, security, abuse prevention, and justified legal retention.
- Successful verification creates a time-limited badge for the institution.
- One institutional email identity cannot verify multiple active accounts.
- Badge expiry or revocation does not remove ordinary account or marketplace access.
- The public badge proves successful verification at a time; it is not a CampusMarkt guarantee of identity, honesty, enrollment duration, item quality, or transaction outcome.

## Reporting

Registered users must be able to report:

- A listing.
- A user account.
- Fraud or attempted fraud.
- Prohibited or unsupported content.
- Harassment, threats, discrimination, spam, or unsafe conduct.
- Privacy exposure or impersonation.

Reports should capture a structured reason and optional context. Reporting must not automatically expose the reporter to the reported user. False or abusive reporting can itself violate the policy.

Emergency or imminent-danger situations belong with the appropriate local emergency or law-enforcement service; CampusMarkt reporting is not an emergency channel.

## Moderation

Basic moderation is part of V1. An approved moderation feature must provide:

- A queue of actionable reports.
- Evidence needed to review the target and its relevant context.
- Authorized actions such as dismissing a report, removing a listing, restricting an account, or escalating a serious case.
- Reason codes and an audit record for privileged actions.
- Least-privilege moderator access.
- Retention and appeal rules approved before launch.

Automated detection may assist later, but it must not be assumed or implemented as an unapproved AI feature.

## Enforcement Outcomes

Depending on severity, evidence, recurrence, and legal obligations, outcomes can include:

- Content correction request.
- Listing removal.
- Temporary marketplace restriction.
- Account suspension or termination.
- Preservation or disclosure required by law.

Exact thresholds, notification, appeals, and retention belong to the moderation feature specification. Moderators must not invent case-specific penalties outside the approved policy and tooling.

## Policy Maintenance

- Product decisions update this document before or with affected feature specs.
- New marketplace verticals require their own policy sections before activation.
- Repeated moderation judgments become explicit policy, not hidden precedent.
- Legal review is required before public launch and after material regulatory or product changes.
- The policy version shown to users must be traceable to the version enforced by moderation.
